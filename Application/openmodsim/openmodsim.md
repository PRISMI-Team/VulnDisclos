# Integer overflow in OpenModSim(CVE-2025-66969)

## Summary

Integer overflow in OpenModSim (<=1.11.0), leading to denial of service

## Affected Product
- Vendor: [OpenModSim](https://github.com/sanny32/OpenModSim)
- Product: OpenModSim
- Affected Versions: <=1.11.0


- 2025/10/10 - Vulnerability discovered during security testing.
- 2025/10/12 - Requests to MITRE for CVE.
- 2026/10/16 - Publicly disclosed
